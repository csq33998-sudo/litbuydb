How It Works

What is a LitBuy Spreadsheet?

A LitBuy Spreadsheet is an organized product discovery resource for shoppers who want quick access to finds, categories, brands, and shopping guide links before ordering through a China shopping agent.

This index focuses on practical routes for Taobao, Weidian, and 1688 discovery. Start with a category, compare product ideas, then open the related LitBuy or MaisonLooks page when you are ready to prepare an order.

Best starting points

Most shoppers begin with shoes, hoodies, T-shirts, jackets, bags, accessories, and budget basics before building a larger haul.

Before ordering

Check product details, estimate shipping weight, review QC photos after warehouse arrival, and compare alternatives if you are choosing between agents.
