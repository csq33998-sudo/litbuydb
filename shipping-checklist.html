LitBuy Shipping Checklist

Use this checklist before submitting a LitBuy parcel or paying international shipping. The goal is to connect the full shopper workflow: LitBuy finds, source links, order notes, warehouse QC photos, parcel restrictions, shipping line choice, address details, and tracking.

Step one Review QC photos

Confirm color, size, quantity, labels, visible defects, packaging, and whether the item matches the source link.

Step two Check parcel rules

Compare line restrictions, estimated weight, volume, packaging, destination coverage, tracking, and delivery speed.

Step three Confirm shipping details

Verify address, declared details, parcel options, payment amount, tracking number, and support records after dispatch.

Before submitting a parcel

  1. Compare QC photos with source links. Open the original source link and confirm the warehouse item matches the expected product, color, size, and visible details.
  2. Check every item status. Do not include an item that still needs return, exchange, clarification, extra photos, or support follow-up.
  3. Estimate parcel weight and volume. Product price is not the full cost. Weight, box size, packaging, and shipping line rules can change the final freight price.
  4. Remove unnecessary packaging if useful. For bulky packaging, decide whether box removal or reinforcement makes sense for the product and shipping line.
  5. Separate risky items. Electronics, batteries, liquids, magnets, fragile goods, oversized boxes, and branded goods may have different line restrictions.

Shipping line checklist

Cost and weight

Compare estimated weight, volume weight, handling options, coupon availability, and whether the cheapest route still fits the item type.

Restrictions

Read line rules for destination country, restricted items, customs requirements, declared value, package size, and prohibited categories.

Delivery and tracking

Compare estimated delivery time, tracking quality, route stability, support expectations, and whether the parcel is time-sensitive.

Address and support

Confirm recipient name, phone, full address, postal code, parcel number, and screenshots needed if support must review the shipment.

After paying shipping

  • Save the parcel number, selected line, payment amount, item list, QC photos, and source links.
  • Track the parcel after dispatch and keep screenshots if tracking stalls or support needs evidence.
  • Use the official LitBuy support route for account, payment, parcel, delivery, refund, or after-sales questions.
