How to use LitBuy finds before ordering

Use this LitBuy finds page as a spreadsheet-style product route before checkout. Start with a product category, open the source link or purchase link, confirm live listing details, and keep the item notes clear enough for warehouse QC photos later.

A useful find should connect discovery with the next steps: source link verification, product option checks, seller notes, QC photos after warehouse arrival, and a shipping checklist before submitting the international parcel.

Source links to verify

Check product title, color, size, stock, domestic shipping, photos, return notes, and whether the source link still matches the spreadsheet label.

QC photos before shipping

After the item reaches the warehouse, compare QC photos with the source link and order notes before choosing a shipping line.
