Weekly update checklist

  1. Remove stale rows. Drop routes where the product page is gone, seller stock is unclear, or the listing no longer matches the spreadsheet label.
  2. Refresh high-demand categories. Prioritize sneakers, hoodies, jerseys, bags, accessories, watches, electronics, and budget basics before niche items.
  3. Check live listing details. Confirm price, color, size, quantity options, domestic shipping, product photos, and seller notes before keeping a route.
  4. Separate budget and premium routes. Do not mix low-cost basics, batch-specific items, and higher-ticket products in the same latest-finds list.
  5. Keep QC as final verification. After ordering, use warehouse photos before international shipping; the latest-finds page should only help users decide what to inspect first.
