LitBuy QC Photos Guide

QC means quality check. For a LitBuy order, the useful QC moment usually happens after the seller sends the item to the LitBuy warehouse and before the shopper submits the international parcel. This page is an independent guide for reading QC photos, comparing warehouse inspection details, and avoiding common order mistakes.

Official service fact Free QC and inspection photos

LitBuy's public service page lists Free QC & Inspection and says items are checked upon arrival with inspection photos provided.

Best use Confirm before shipping

Use QC photos to verify color, size label, visible defects, packaging, and whether the item matches the order notes before paying international shipping.

Limit Photos reduce risk, not all risk

A warehouse photo can reveal obvious issues, but it cannot guarantee fit, fabric feel, long-term durability, customs handling, or seller after-sales outcome.

What the platform confirms

LitBuy describes itself as a China shopping agent and its public app text references marketplace routes such as 1688, Taobao, Weidian, and JD. For QC specifically, the practical takeaway is simple: the inspection happens after warehouse arrival. You should make the final ship-or-return decision before submitting the parcel, because international shipping creates another cost layer and usually makes after-sales handling harder.

How to read LitBuy QC photos

  1. Match the order basics. Check product title, color, size, quantity, seller notes, and any variant options you selected.
  2. Inspect visible details. Zoom into logo placement, stitching, print alignment, labels, hardware, soles, zippers, buttons, and packaging condition.
  3. Look for shipping risks. Heavy boxes, fragile parts, liquids, batteries, magnets, and oversized packaging can affect line availability or final cost.
  4. Act before parcel submission. If the QC photos show an issue, request clarification, return, exchange, or extra photos before paying international freight.
  5. Keep records. Save the original listing, seller messages, order notes, and QC photos in case support needs context later.

QC checker workflow

Use QC for

  • Checking whether the warehouse item matches the selected product option.
  • Spotting obvious stains, scratches, misprints, missing parts, or damaged packaging.
  • Comparing seller photos with warehouse photos before international shipping.
  • Deciding whether a return, exchange, or extra photo request is worth the delay.

Do not use QC as

  • A guarantee that sizing will fit your body or feet perfectly.
  • A promise that customs, delivery speed, or tax outcomes will be predictable.
  • A substitute for reading the original product listing and seller return rules.
  • A reason to ignore restricted-item notices, shipping line rules, or payment policies.

Common LitBuy QC mistakes

  • Approving photos too quickly without checking the selected size or color.
  • Comparing only the front photo and ignoring tags, soles, sleeves, zippers, labels, or back views.
  • Waiting too long to request a return or exchange after a warehouse issue appears.
  • Judging a haul only by item price while ignoring parcel weight, volume, coupons, and route restrictions.
